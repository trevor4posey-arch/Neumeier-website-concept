          Questions operators ask

          Straight answers before you call.

          Still not covered? The warehouse line is answered around the clock.

          Call 806-323-3490
          Where do you work?

          Oklahoma, the Texas Panhandle, West Texas and New Mexico, from rail-served sites in Canadian, Clinton, Chickasha and Odessa.

          Do you use third-party vendors?

          No. Supply chain, blending, logistics and field support are all in-house, so one company is accountable from the plant to the wellsite.

          How fast can I get product on location?

          The warehouse answers 24/7 and bulk LCM is available immediately. Typical dispatch time by yard: company to supply

          What wells and hole sizes do you support?

          Vertical and horizontal wells, including laterals of two miles and longer. Programs are built around 6.125", 6.75" and 8.75" production holes.

          Do you sell to other mud companies?

          Yes. Neumeier supplies LCM and specialty materials to other mud companies as a regional bulk and blending partner.

          What should I send for an estimate?

          Your drilling plan or wellbore diagram, the formation, hole sizes and expected spud date. The request form takes all of it in one step.
